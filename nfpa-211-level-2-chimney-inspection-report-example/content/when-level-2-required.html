When is a Level 2 Inspection Required?

NFPA 211 Requirements

According to NFPA 211, a Level 2 inspection is required upon:

  • Sale or transfer of property
  • Change of fuel type (e.g., wood to gas)
  • Change to shape or material of the flue liner
  • Replacement or addition of an appliance
  • After an event likely to have caused damage (chimney fire, earthquake, lightning strike)
  • External evidence of damage to chimney or structure
  • Problems with the system suspected but not confirmed by Level 1

Real Estate Transactions

Level 2 inspections are particularly important during real estate transactions because they provide a comprehensive assessment of the chimney system's condition, helping both buyers and sellers understand any potential issues or necessary repairs.

After Chimney Events

Following a chimney fire, severe weather event, or any incident that may have damaged the chimney, a Level 2 inspection with video scanning is essential to identify hidden damage that could pose future fire or carbon monoxide risks.
