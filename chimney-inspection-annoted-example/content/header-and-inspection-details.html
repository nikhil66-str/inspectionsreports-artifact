Header and Inspection Details

The header section establishes the essential identification information for the inspection.

Required Header Information

  • Company Logo & Name - Professional branding
  • Report Title - Type of inspection performed
  • Property Address - Complete address of inspected property
  • Inspection Date - Date inspection was performed
  • Client Name - Who requested the inspection
  • Report Number - Unique identifier for tracking

Report Numbering Best Practices

Use a consistent numbering system such as:

  • Level-Year-Sequence (e.g., L2-2026-0042)
  • Date-based (e.g., 20260201-001)
  • Sequential (e.g., INS-2026-042)

Why This Matters

Clear identification information helps with record-keeping, follow-up communications, and potential legal proceedings.
