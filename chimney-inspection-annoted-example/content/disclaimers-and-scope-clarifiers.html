Disclaimers and Scope Clarifiers

Proper disclaimer language is essential for legal protection. Consult with an attorney to ensure your disclaimers are appropriate for your jurisdiction.

Standard Disclaimer Elements

  • Scope Statement: What was and wasn't included in the inspection
  • Point-in-Time: Conditions reflect the inspection date only
  • Standards Referenced: NFPA 211, CSIA, or other applicable standards
  • Non-Code Compliance: Statement that this is not a code compliance audit
  • Level Boundaries: Clear distinction between Level 1, 2, and 3 inspections

Sample Disclaimer Language

"This inspection is limited to readily accessible and visible components at the time of inspection. Concealed conditions, underground components, or areas requiring destructive investigation are beyond the scope of this report."

Level 3 Boundary Statement

Always clarify that destructive testing and concealed analysis require a Level 3 inspection, which this report does not include.
