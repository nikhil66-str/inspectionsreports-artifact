Introduction: Why Narrative Language Matters

The language used in an NFPA 211 Level 2 chimney inspection report can be the difference between professional protection and personal liability. Every word matters.

Precise wording protects inspectors from misinterpretation and liability claims
Clear language demonstrates adherence to NFPA 211 standards and best practices
Accurate narratives set proper expectations and build trust with clients

This guide provides safe, defensible language examples for NFPA 211 Level 2 inspection reports, helping inspectors communicate findings clearly while maintaining professional boundaries.
