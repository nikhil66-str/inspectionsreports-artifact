Documentation Scope

What Must Be Documented

A comprehensive Level 2 inspection report should document:

  • Property and client identification
  • Date and scope of inspection
  • Chimney construction type and materials
  • Appliance connections and compatibility
  • Flue liner condition (via video scan)
  • Clearances to combustible materials
  • Structural integrity observations
  • Safety hazards identified
  • Recommendations and required repairs

Areas of Inspection

  • Firebox and damper assembly
  • Smoke chamber
  • Flue liner (all sections)
  • Crown and cap
  • Chimney chase (if accessible)
  • Attic penetration areas
  • Crawl space areas (if applicable)
  • Exterior masonry/siding interface

Documentation Standards

All documentation should be:

  • Clear and professionally written
  • Supported by photographic evidence
  • Referenced to NFPA 211 standards where applicable
  • Signed by a certified inspector
