Limitations & Scope Language

Properly documenting limitations is just as important as documenting findings. Clear scope language protects both the inspector and the client.

Standard Limitation Statement

This inspection is limited to readily accessible and visible components at the time of inspection. The following are beyond the scope of this Level 2 inspection:

  • Concealed structural components requiring removal of finishes
  • Underground or buried chimney foundations
  • Interior wall cavities and chases not visible via video scan
  • Future performance or conditions not present at time of inspection
  • Code compliance verification (municipal codes vary by jurisdiction)
  • Destructive testing or material sampling

Level 2 vs. Level 3 Boundary

When to Recommend Level 3

Level 3 inspections involve destructive investigation and are only recommended when serious hazards are suspected. Use this language:

"Evaluation of the suspected condition at [location] would require removal of [finishes/materials], which exceeds the scope of this Level 2 inspection. If serious hazards are suspected, a Level 3 inspection per NFPA 211 may be warranted."

Area-Specific Limitation Examples

Area Limitation Language
Attic Access "Attic access was limited due to low clearance and insulation depth. Only visible portions were examined from the access opening."
Video Scan "Video scan examination was performed to the extent possible. Camera articulation limitations prevented complete visualization of the upper smoke chamber."
Exterior Roof "Exterior chimney inspection from roof level was not performed due to steep pitch and safety concerns. Drone or lift equipment would be required for complete exterior evaluation."
Finished Walls "Clearances behind finished drywall at chimney chase were not verified. Destructive investigation would be required to confirm compliance."
Foundation "Below-grade chimney foundation was not accessible for inspection. No evaluation of underground structural components was performed."

Scope Definition Template

Use this template structure for scope sections:

  1. State the standard: "This inspection was performed in accordance with NFPA 211 Level 2 standards."
  2. List what WAS inspected: "The following components were examined: [list]"
  3. List what was NOT accessible: "The following areas were not accessible or are beyond scope: [list]"
  4. Reference future conditions: "This report represents conditions at the time of inspection. Continued annual inspections per NFPA 211 are recommended."

Access & Exception Statement

Standard Language

"All readily accessible and safely accessible areas were inspected during this evaluation. Areas that were not accessible due to structural obstructions, safety concerns, finished construction, or physical limitations are documented in the limitations section where applicable. No destructive testing or removal of building materials was performed."

Best Practice

Document limitations at the time of inspection, not after the fact. If you couldn't access an area, note it in your field notes immediately. This prevents "why didn't you check that?" questions later.
