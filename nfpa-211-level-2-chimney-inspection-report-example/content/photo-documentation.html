Photo Documentation Best Practices

Why Photos Matter

Photographic documentation provides:

  • Visual evidence supporting written findings
  • Legal protection for all parties
  • Baseline for future comparisons
  • Clear communication of conditions

Required Photos

A comprehensive Level 2 report should include photos of:

  • Chimney exterior (overall and close-ups)
  • Crown and cap condition
  • Flashing and roof interface
  • Firebox and damper
  • Smoke chamber (if visible)
  • Video scan stills of significant findings
  • All clearance measurement locations
  • Any deficiencies identified

Photo Quality Standards

  • Resolution - Minimum 2MP, recommended 5MP+
  • Lighting - Use flash or auxiliary lighting
  • Focus - Clear, sharp images
  • Scale - Include ruler for measurements when applicable

Labeling and Organization

Best practices for photo organization:

  • Number photos sequentially
  • Include descriptive captions
  • Reference photos in written findings
  • Group by location or issue type
