Limitations and Conditions

Documenting limitations protects both the inspector and the client by establishing clear boundaries of what was and wasn't inspected.

Common Limitation Categories

  • Physical Access: Areas blocked by furniture, storage, or structural elements
  • Safety Limitations: Roof pitch, weather conditions, structural concerns
  • Equipment Limitations: Camera reach, articulation in tight spaces
  • Concealed Areas: Areas behind walls, in chase enclosures

Documentation Format

For each limitation, document:

  • Area: Specific location that couldn't be fully inspected
  • Reason: Why access was limited
  • Impact: How this affects the inspection completeness

Why This Matters

Proper limitation documentation prevents claims that you missed something when you clearly documented you couldn't access that area.
