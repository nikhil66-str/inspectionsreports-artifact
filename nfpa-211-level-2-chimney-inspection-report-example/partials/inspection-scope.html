Inspection Scope

This Level 2 inspection was conducted in accordance with NFPA 211 Standard for Chimneys, Fireplaces, Vents, and Solid Fuel-Burning Appliances.

Reason for Level 2 Inspection

Areas Inspected

  • Readily accessible portions of chimney exterior and interior
  • Video scan examination of flue interior surfaces
  • Accessible attic spaces and chimney penetrations
  • Accessible crawl space areas (if applicable)
  • Clearances to combustible materials
  • Proper construction and connections
  • Connected appliance and firebox

Equipment Used

  • Video camera inspection system
  • Flashlight and mirrors
  • Level and measuring tools
  • Digital camera for documentation
