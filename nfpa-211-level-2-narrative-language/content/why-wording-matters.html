Why Wording Matters

Legal Implications

  • Reports are legal documents that can be used in court
  • Overstating findings can lead to liability claims
  • Guaranteeing outcomes creates contractual obligations
  • Vague language allows multiple interpretations

Professional Standards

  • NFPA 211 requires specific documentation
  • Industry organizations expect consistent terminology
  • Insurance carriers review language for coverage
  • Licensing boards may investigate poor documentation

Client Communication

  • Clear language prevents misunderstandings
  • Proper scope statements manage expectations
  • Accurate narratives support informed decisions
  • Professional tone builds confidence

Professional Insight

Most liability issues in chimney inspection arise not from technical errors, but from communication failures in written reports. The difference between "This chimney is safe" and "No deficiencies were observed during this inspection" is legally significant.
