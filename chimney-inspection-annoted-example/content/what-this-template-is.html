What This Template Is

This is an annotated example of a chimney inspection report template. It's designed to show what information goes where and explain why each section is important.

This Template Is:

  • An educational guide to report structure
  • A reference for creating your own templates
  • An explanation of best practices in documentation
  • A tool for training new inspectors

This Template Is NOT:

  • A substitute for proper training and certification
  • Legal advice (consult an attorney for legal matters)
  • A complete inspection methodology guide
  • A code compliance checklist

How to Use This Template

Review each section to understand its purpose, then adapt the structure and language to fit your specific needs and the requirements of your inspection software or documentation system.
