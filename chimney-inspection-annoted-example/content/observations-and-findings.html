Observations and Findings

The findings section documents what was observed during the inspection, organized by component or area.

Standard Rating Categories

  • Satisfactory: Meets standards, no visible defects
  • Needs Attention: Minor issues, not immediate safety concern
  • Deficient: Significant issues, requires repair before use

Components to Document

  • Chimney crown and cap
  • Flue liner condition
  • Smoke chamber
  • Damper operation
  • Firebox condition
  • Clearances to combustibles
  • Exterior masonry
  • Flashing and waterproofing

Documentation Best Practices

  • Be specific about location and extent of issues
  • Reference photo numbers for visual evidence
  • Use consistent terminology throughout
  • Document both positive and negative findings
