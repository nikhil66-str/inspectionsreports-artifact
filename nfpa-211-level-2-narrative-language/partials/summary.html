Example Summary Language

Clean Report Summary

"The chimney system serving the masonry fireplace was inspected in accordance with NFPA 211 Level 2 standards. A video scan of the flue interior was performed, and accessible portions of the attic, basement, and exterior were examined. No deficiencies requiring immediate attention were observed at the time of inspection. Routine maintenance and annual Level 1 inspections are recommended per NFPA 211."

Report with Findings Summary

"Level 2 video scan inspection revealed creosote deposits exceeding NFPA 211 cleaning thresholds and a separation in the clay tile liner at the smoke chamber transition. Clearances to combustibles in accessible areas meet NFPA 211 requirements. Professional cleaning and evaluation of the liner condition are recommended prior to further use."
