Limitations & Disclaimers

Scope of Inspection

A Level 2 inspection is limited to:

  • Readily accessible areas without dismantling
  • Visual examination with approved equipment
  • Conditions existing at time of inspection
  • Areas not concealed by construction

Areas Beyond Scope

The following are not included in a Level 2 inspection:

  • Concealed components requiring demolition to access
  • Underground portions of systems
  • Performance testing (smoke/draft tests unless specified)
  • Conditions that develop after inspection date
  • Areas made inaccessible by stored items

Standard Disclaimer Language

"This inspection report represents the condition of the chimney and venting system at the time of inspection. The inspector is not responsible for conditions that develop after the inspection date or for concealed defects that cannot be observed through standard Level 2 inspection methods. This report is prepared in accordance with NFPA 211 and represents the professional opinion of the certified inspector."

Recommendations for Level 3

A Level 3 inspection may be recommended when:

  • Concealed damage is suspected
  • Destructive investigation is required
  • Previous repairs need verification
  • Hazard cannot be fully evaluated by Level 2 methods
