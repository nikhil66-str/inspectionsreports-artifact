Level 1 vs Level 2 Inspection Comparison

Understanding the Differences

Aspect Level 1 Level 2
Scope Readily accessible areas All accessible areas including attic, crawl spaces
Video Scan Not included Required for flue interior
Clearances Visual check where accessible All accessible clearances verified
Documentation Basic findings report Comprehensive report with photos
When Required Annual maintenance Property sale, appliance changes, after events

Level 1 Inspection

A Level 1 inspection is appropriate for routine maintenance when:

  • No changes have been made to the system
  • The chimney has been used as expected
  • No known problems exist

Level 2 Inspection

A Level 2 inspection provides a more thorough evaluation including:

  • All Level 1 components
  • Accessible portions of chimney exterior and interior
  • Accessible portions of connected appliance(s)
  • Video inspection of flue interior surfaces
  • Verification of proper clearances to combustibles
