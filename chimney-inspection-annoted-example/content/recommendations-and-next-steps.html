Recommendations and Next Steps

Clear, prioritized recommendations help clients understand what actions to take and in what order.

Priority Levels

  • Immediate: Safety hazard, do not use system until addressed
  • Before Next Use: Should be completed before operating the system
  • Seasonal: Address before next heating season
  • Annual Maintenance: Include in regular maintenance schedule
  • Monitor: Watch for changes at next inspection

Recommendation Writing Tips

  • Be specific about what needs to be done
  • Explain why the action is important
  • Reference specific findings that prompted the recommendation
  • Suggest appropriate professionals when specialized work is needed

Avoid Common Pitfalls

  • Don't be vague ("repair as needed")
  • Don't recommend specific contractors (liability concern)
  • Don't provide cost estimates unless specifically requested
