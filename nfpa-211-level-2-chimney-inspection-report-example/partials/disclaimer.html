Limitations & Disclaimers

Scope of Inspection

This Level 2 inspection is limited to the readily accessible and visible areas of the chimney and venting system at the time of inspection. The inspection includes a video examination of the flue interior and examination of accessible portions of the chimney structure including accessible attic, crawl space, and basement areas.

Limitations

  • Concealed conditions within walls, foundations, or areas requiring destructive investigation are beyond the scope of this inspection.
  • Performance testing (draft, smoke tests) is not included unless specifically requested and noted.
  • Underground portions of systems, if any, are not accessible for inspection.
  • Areas blocked by stored items, insulation, or other obstructions may not be fully inspected.
  • This inspection does not guarantee future performance or condition of the system.

Standard of Care

This inspection has been performed in accordance with NFPA 211 and represents the professional opinion of a certified chimney inspector. The findings are based on conditions observed at the time of inspection using standard Level 2 inspection methods.

Recommendations

If concealed damage is suspected or if conditions warrant more detailed examination, a Level 3 inspection involving selective removal of components may be recommended. Any repairs should be performed by qualified professionals in accordance with applicable codes and standards.

This report is prepared exclusively for the client named herein and may not be used by any other party for any purpose without written consent of the inspecting company. Information in this report may not be reproduced without permission.
