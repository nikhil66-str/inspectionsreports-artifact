Introduction to the Annotated Template

This annotated template provides a comprehensive guide to creating professional chimney inspection reports. Each section explains what information should be included and why it matters for legal protection, client communication, and industry compliance.

Purpose of This Guide

Whether you're a new chimney inspector building your first report template, or an experienced professional looking to refine your documentation, this guide breaks down each component of a professional inspection report.

Key Principles

  • Clarity: Every section should be easy for clients to understand
  • Completeness: Document all findings, both positive and negative
  • Consistency: Use the same format and terminology throughout
  • Compliance: Align with NFPA 211 and CSIA standards
  • Protection: Include proper disclaimer and limitation language
