Clearance to Combustibles Reporting

NFPA 211 Clearance Requirements

NFPA 211 specifies minimum clearances between chimney components and combustible materials to prevent fire hazards:

  • Masonry chimneys - 2 inches to combustibles
  • Factory-built chimneys - Per manufacturer specifications
  • Connectors - 18 inches standard (6-9 inches with protection)

Areas to Inspect

Clearances must be verified in all accessible areas:

  • Attic penetration points
  • Floor penetration points
  • Wall pass-throughs
  • Ceiling joist proximity
  • Roof framing near chimney
  • Insulation placement

Documentation Format

Clearance reporting should include:

Location Required Actual Status
Attic - North Side 2" 2.5" Compliant
Attic - South Side 2" 1.5" Non-Compliant

Common Violations

  • Insulation touching chimney masonry
  • Wood framing too close at penetrations
  • Combustible storage against chimney in attic
  • Improper firestop installation
