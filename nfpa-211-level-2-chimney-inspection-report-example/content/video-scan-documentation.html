Video Scan Documentation

Purpose of Video Scanning

Video scanning allows inspectors to examine the entire interior surface of the flue, identifying issues that cannot be seen from above or below:

  • Cracks in flue liner
  • Missing or damaged mortar joints
  • Creosote buildup levels
  • Obstructions (debris, animal nests)
  • Deterioration of liner materials
  • Evidence of previous repairs

Flue Zones

The flue is typically divided into zones for documentation:

  • Smoke Chamber - Area above damper
  • Lower Flue - First third of flue height
  • Middle Flue - Center section
  • Upper Flue - Upper third approaching crown
  • Crown Area - Termination point

Documentation Requirements

Video scan documentation should include:

  • Still images of significant findings
  • Description of conditions by zone
  • Measurements where applicable
  • Comparison to previous inspections if available

Common Findings

  • Stage 1-3 creosote deposits
  • Liner cracks and gaps
  • Water damage evidence
  • Smoke chamber pargeting condition
  • Crown deterioration visible from inside
